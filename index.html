Agile development methodology

Jira was used during project execution for Scrum backlog and sprint planning. EduSprint is the resulting standalone application.

Backlog planningUser stories, tasks and risk-related work were organized into the Scrum backlog.
Sprint executionWork was grouped into short iterations to manage development progress incrementally.
Task trackingDevelopment work was tracked through statuses and sprint-level priorities.
Risk reviewProject risks were identified, assessed and prioritized alongside development work.
